Harvie M. Conn (1933–1999)

Missionary to Korea, Westminster Theological Seminary's first professor of missions, and one of the people who called evangelicals to the city. This page is where I listen to his lectures, write down what I'm learning, and watch my understanding grow.

Where your notes live

Your notes, progress, and recordings are saved in this browser. Clearing site data or switching devices will lose them unless you export a backup. Audio files you upload are kept in the browser too, but they aren't included in the backup file. Links are.

Export / import

To publish your notes with the site, export the file, name it harvie-conn-data.json, and commit it next to this page. Any visitor (or you, on another device) will see those notes too.
